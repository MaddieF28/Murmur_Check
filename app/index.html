How it works

  1. You record the heart for 15 seconds at three spots: the top and bottom of the breastbone, and under the chest muscle. The AI learned from CirCor, a public collection of heart recordings from about 1,500 children in Brazil, recorded with a digital stethoscope.
  2. The app checks that each recording holds a steady heartbeat and is mostly heart sound. If it doesn't, the app asks you to record again instead of guessing.
  3. It turns each recording into a picture of how loud each pitch is over time, the heat map on the results screen. Five small neural networks look at that picture for the sound of a murmur, and their answers are averaged. If any spot scores past the line, the app says to get an echocardiogram.
  4. A phone hears the heart differently from a stethoscope, muffling the higher sounds. We recorded hearts with phones, measured how much they muffle each pitch, and changed the stethoscope recordings to sound the same way. The networks learned from those changed recordings, so they work from a phone's microphone.
  5. Everything runs on this phone. Recordings are never uploaded.

Safeguards

  • It won't score a recording it can't hear clearly. It asks for a new one instead.
  • One recording that sounds like a murmur is enough to recommend getting an echocardiogram.
  • It reports "no murmur heard", not that the heart is healthy, because a recording can miss other heart problems.
  • All your information is safe, and no information leaves the phone.

How it compares

Previous ways to detect heart murmurs from heart sounds cost up to $500, like Eko's digital stethoscope, and need a trained doctor. Murmur Check is free, runs on your own phone, and is built for children. It lets people with no medical training check for a heart murmur in about a minute. Everything runs on the phone, so once the page is open it needs no internet.

The model is tiny: this 3 MB model is as accurate as a published 87-million-parameter model at two U.S. children's heart clinics, while being about 150 times smaller.
